about

e-GoS (Gabriel Vianello) is a multi-disciplinary artist based in St. Louis, Missouri. The producer performs experimental jazz and electronic music, often accompanied by dancers who help guide the experience into the realm of performance art. Always on a creative pursuit, e-GoS has been known to collaborate musically with St. Louis-based dance companies such as the Resilience Dance Company and Karlovsky Company Dance. As an all-around creative, e-GoS also experiments in the world of videography and visual art, enhancing his performances, as well as collaborating with other artists. The twenty-five year old artist isn't afraid to explore new realms of creative expression, always evoking a sense of wonder and playfulness in his work.
